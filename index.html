Fabrica Videos

A private, internal tool that uploads and schedules videos on the YouTube channels operated by its owner.

What it does

  • Uploads video files that the owner has produced to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, category, language and scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail of each uploaded video.
  • Reads the name and ID of the authorized channel, only to confirm that each video goes to the right channel.

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.
  • It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or channel.
  • It does not delete videos, post or manage comments, or change account settings.

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
